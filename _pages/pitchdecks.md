ToxQuest: ToxQuest is a decision-support platform that helps early-stage drug developers transition confidently from in silico modeling to pre-clinical testing. Existing toxicity AI models in the drug toxicology space (e.g., DeepTox, ADMETlab) produce complex predicitons that leave early-stage teams overwhelmed. ToxQuest integrates these models into a single platform using a game theory–based coordination algorithm called "satisficing paths". Rather than producing more scores, ToxQuest delivers a clear, and explainable shortlist of the safest candidate molecules. This saves pre-clinical teams time and money on potentially failed drugs downstream. Our first users will be academic drug discovery labs, CROs, and early-stage biotech teams screening 10–500 compounds. We aim to validate performance using compounds with known outcomes, then scale as a lean, acquisition-ready platform.
