**ToxQuest:**

ToxQuest is a decision-support platform designed to help early-stage drug discovery teams confidently transition from in silico modeling to preclinical testing. Today, there are so many different options for AI toxicity prediction tools, but they are fragmented. Different models return different types of outputs, often leaving early teams overwhelmed, having to cross-reference outputs for 100s of molecules and unsure of how to proceed. 
ToxQuest aims to addresses this by integrating multiple open-source AI toxicity models into one unified platform,  to resolve conflicts and highlight consensus. Instead of producing more numerical scores, ToxQuest aims to deliver an explainable, ranked shortlist of the safest candidate molecules to proceed with into drug synthesis and preclinical experiments. This allows users to prioritize compounds more effectively and reduce the time and cost associated with finding out their drugs are unsafe during costly experimental phases. Our technical differentiation lies in how we will synthesize diverse model outputs into a single, interpretable decision layer- built specifically for drug developers on small teams who might not have both toxicology AND ML expertise.
Our goal is to have our first users be academic labs, CROs, and early-stage biotech teams screening 10 to 500 compounds during hit prioritization or lead optimization. 
We are currently exiting the ideation phase and entering the early build phase; developing our MVP, validation dataset, and conducting product-market research. The long-term goal is to build a lean, acquisition-ready platform that integrates seamlessly into early-stage discovery pipelines.
